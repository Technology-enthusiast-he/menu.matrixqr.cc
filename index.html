Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our menu image to QR code generator.

Is this menu QR code generator free?

Yes, it is 100% free with no hidden fees or subscription required. You can generate unlimited QR codes for your restaurant or cafe menus.

Does the generated menu QR code expire?

No, the generated QR codes do not expire. Once generated, your customers can scan and view your menu anytime.

Can I edit text or prices on my menu here?

This tool converts existing menu images (JPG, PNG, WEBP) into QR codes. It is not an online menu editor. To update your menu, simply upload a new menu image and generate a new QR code.

What menu image formats are supported?

We support JPG, PNG, and WEBP image formats up to 10MB per file. All images are hosted securely on fast cloud storage.

Can I add my restaurant logo to the QR code?

Yes! You can upload your store logo in the generator options. The logo processing happens entirely client-side in your browser for fast and reliable rendering.
